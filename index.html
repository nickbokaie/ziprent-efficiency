Department collaboration — escalation triage structure
We have made deliberate efforts to have departments handle escalated issues in a structured, tiered fashion rather than defaulting to Escalations immediately. The process works as follows:
1
Issue surfaces
An escalated issue is identified within the relevant department.
→
2
Team Lead reviews & de-escalates
The Team Lead is the first line of defense — reviews the situation and attempts to de-escalate directly.
→
3
Department Manager judgment call
If unresolved, it escalates to the Department Manager who assesses severity and determines next steps.
→
4
Josh (Escalations) — last line of defense
Only if the issue remains unresolved does the Department Manager loop in Josh from Escalations as the final escalation point.
